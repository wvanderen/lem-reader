# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Readers of saved long-form web articles, especially people who benefit from reduced distraction, stable spatial orientation, predictable navigation, and adaptable presentation. They use Lem Reader for focused reading across desktop and mobile contexts.

## Product Purpose

Lem Reader presents normalized articles in calm paginated and scrolling views while preserving rich semantic structure, reading location, highlights, and notes. Success means readers can move through long-form content with reliable orientation and without layout churn or mode lock-in.

## Brand Personality

Calm, booklike, and dependable. The interface should recede behind the article while remaining legible and reassuring whenever the reader needs orientation or controls.

## Anti-references

Avoid attention-seeking reading chrome, dense dashboard styling, magazine-like visual fragmentation, unstable animated layout, canvas-rendered prose, tiny controls, and pagination that overrides reader choice or accessibility needs.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the article primary and make controls quietly discoverable.
2. Preserve stable spatial orientation across page turns, resizing, and mode changes.
3. Prefer familiar native interaction patterns and semantic structure.
4. Adapt the reading surface to the reader instead of enforcing a single mode.
5. Treat predictable behavior and technical reliability as part of the visual experience.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Semantic HTML, keyboard navigation, screen-reader compatibility, browser zoom and reflow, visible focus, minimum comfortable touch targets, and reduced-motion behavior are foundational requirements. Paginated and scrolling modes must both remain available, and local position/annotation persistence must not depend on derived page numbers.
