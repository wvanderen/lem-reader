This blocks ACPT-08's zero-major VoiceOver+Safari arm (Phase 21 success criterion 5).

Full diagnosis: .planning/debug/vo-safari-image-page-focus.md (probe scripts verified the full chain: chevrons call commitTurn directly; WebKit click leaves activeElement === body; isFocusInContent(body) === false cascades; figure-only page makes the Next-page button the natural VO turn path).
