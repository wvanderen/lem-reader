## Phase Goal

**As a** reader, **I want to** read and resume articles in a calm scrolling view with adaptable typography, theme, and recoverable local preferences, **so that** the reading experience matches my access needs and I never lose my place.

> Plan 03 completes the slice: **resumable reading + quiet structural progress**. The reader reopens an article and lands at their saved spot; a quiet hairline + heading-change announce track progress without page-number identity.

<objective>
Deliver STATE-01 (location restore) on the Phase-1 grapheme-offset substrate (D-05): persist reading location as a grapheme offset over `normalizeText(article)` keyed `[articleId+revision]`; on reopen, resolve the offset to a DOM block via `findScrollTarget` (reusing normalizeText's per-block rules exactly) and silently scroll there, then surface a dismissible resume banner. Add READ-05 + A11Y-08 quiet progress: a 2px aria-hidden `scaleX` hairline (no transition) under the header + a polite live region announcing section changes (IntersectionObserver scroll-spy, debounced against flooding — Pitfall 6). Scroll-save is debounced (~1200ms) with the same bfcache-safe dual flush as settings.

Purpose: STATE-01, READ-05, A11Y-08; completes STATE-04 (location store validation). Mounts in `ArticleView` (the one route Plan 01/02 left untouched).
Output: locationStore + restoreLocation + useScrollSave + ProgressHairline + SectionAnnouncer + ResumeBanner; ArticleView wired; green locationSchema/restoreLocation unit tests + section-announce/progress/location-persistence e2e.
</objective>
