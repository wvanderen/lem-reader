## Phase Goal (User Story)

**As a** reader who values stable, faithful content,
**I want to** open representative saved articles whose semantic structure, provenance, and logical text coordinates are explicit and verifiable,
**so that** every later reading, location, and annotation feature can trust the foundation.

This plan delivers the **frozen contract half** of that story: the runnable project, the locked document model (D-04, D-06), and the grapheme-offset substrate (D-05) that Phase 2 (location) and Phase 5 (annotations) persist against.

## <objective>
Establish the greenfield Vite 8 + React 19 + TypeScript 7 scaffold with full test infrastructure, then lock the two irreversible Phase 1 contracts: the Zod document model (identity + revision + discriminated-union blocks) and the deterministic grapheme-cluster coordinate system. Both are unit-tested from day one because changing them later corrupts every saved location and highlight.

Purpose: This plan exists because Phase 2 and Phase 5 persist offsets and identities against contracts that cannot change after release. Locking them first — with regression tests for every documented pitfall — is the highest-leverage work in Phase 1.

Output: A runnable, lint-clean, test-green project skeleton plus the frozen `schema.ts`, `types.ts`, `normalizeText.ts`, and reserved `db.ts` modules with their unit-test guards.
</objective>
