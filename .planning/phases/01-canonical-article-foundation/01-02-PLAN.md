## Phase Goal (User Story)

**As a** reader who values stable, faithful content,
**I want to** open representative saved articles whose semantic structure, provenance, and logical text coordinates are explicit and verifiable,
**so that** every later reading, location, and annotation feature can trust the foundation.

This plan delivers the **walking-skeleton UI half** of that story: the in-memory repository round-trip (the A3 deviation from the generic DB read/write), the recursive semantic renderer, the hash-routed two-view app, and the warm-paper token system. After this plan, a reader can open one seed article and read its semantic content as native HTML.

## <objective>
Wire the frozen contracts from Plan 01 into a working end-to-end vertical slice: in-memory ArticleRepository (Phase 2 swaps in Dexie behind the same interface) → recursive semantic renderer (DOC-02 reading order) → hash-routed FixtureList + ArticleView (DOC-01, DOC-03) with the DOC-06 unsupported-content disclosure rendered inline. The warm-paper D-07 visual defaults and defensive a11y media-query blocks land here. This completes the Walking Skeleton.

Purpose: This plan proves the full stack works end-to-end on one seed article before Plan 03 expands to the curated corpus. It also establishes the rendering and routing patterns every later phase extends.

Output: A runnable app at http://localhost:5173 where a reader can open one seed article and read its semantic content; component tests assert every block kind renders its native element.
</objective>
