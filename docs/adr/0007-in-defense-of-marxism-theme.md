# The "In Defense of Marxism" specialty rooms ride the wash hooks

The reader asked for a specialty theme carrying a darker red into the wayfinder chrome and other UI elements, then a dark companion. Rather than a new mechanism, the rooms ride the ADR 0006 discipline exactly: hand-authored `[data-theme]` blocks (`marxism-light`, `marxism-dark`), an additive enum widening (no schemaVersion bump, no stored token record, no superRefine rule), the pre-paint mirror map extended with both keys, and every policed pair pinned by the extraction-anchor tests (`prideThemes.test.ts` audits four blocks; the highlight AA extraction map extends to both).

The light room is the pamphlet reading room: a reader works through long-form theory and history on a lit desk, and the red belongs to the chrome the way a pamphlet's covers belong to its pages. Deep oxblood enamel owns the band (`#3f141b` — the darker-red wayfinding), the lit board steps to `#5b2029`, links go crimson (`#a01c2e`), the metal turns red-brown (`#8f3d33` on paper, `#d08078` lit), and the paper takes a warm newsprint cast (`#f8f3f1`). The night room is the companion — the banner stays lit after close, built on the Night register's disciplines: red-black walls (`#1d1315`), a near-black oxblood band (`#150a0d`), links lightened to rose-crimson (`#e08692`) for dark-surface AA, solid fills pinned dark (`#4a161d`), the metal brightened to read on dark walls (`#d98b87` / `#e8a49b`), and hue-true deep marker fills carrying the light ink. The Brightest Panel rule holds in both rooms: the red is committed to the chrome; the article measure stays the brightest, quietest surface. Destructive keeps the system pair (`#a63d32` light / `#e08a80` dark): the identity red is darker and crimson-leaning, and the destructive vocabulary carries its state by treatment (tint + border), never by hue alone.

There is no flag to blend, so the wash is the rooms' banner: `--flag-wash` is a single-hue blend (deep red → lit red) riding the same two moments — the progress hairline and the lit current-location underline — with the band edge staying the basic lit metal. The night room's wash brightens one step (`#a51b28 → #d8434f`) so the moving hairline stays visible on dark walls. The ONE-gradient-material rule is unchanged; additional rooms joining the exception are an extension of ADR 0006's precedent, not a new one. Forced-colors still strips the image; the solid marks stay painted beneath it.
